# v2.3.0 - Safety supervision and audit fixes

This release adds runtime supervision, makes crashes and corrupt data visible, and fixes the findings from a full firmware audit. It requires no hardware changes. **It has not been tested on the assembled machine, so run the checks below before welding with it.**

## Safety

- The motor stops and **HMI STALE** latches if the LVGL loop stalls for 2 s or touch fails 100 reads in a row. At rest, new starts are blocked instead.
- A pot jump to the rail latches **SPEED POT** after three samples. Turning the knob to zero by hand is unaffected.
- A panic, watchdog or brownout restart latches a visible fault at boot. The event log survives these resets, but not power loss.
- Invalid saved data boots to a visible **STORAGE** fault with motion disabled and the data untouched.
- Settings and programs are saved only in IDLE or ESTOP, and a start waits for a save in progress.

## Changes that affect how the machine runs

- **RPM cap per diameter:** workpiece RPM is limited by `MAX_STEP_HZ` (200 kHz) and `MOTOR_RPM_LIMIT` (1500 RPM). Saved programs above the cap are blocked at start. The 1500 RPM limit is a chosen value, not a measured one.
- **Acceleration:** the setting now means steps/s2 at 1/16. Ramps at 1/16 are unchanged; other microstep settings get a different ramp.
- **Step mode** holds torque during the dwell, so ENA stays asserted.
- **Pulse mode** latches direction at start.
- **Restarts:** a restart after a crash or brownout requires acknowledgement.

## Added

- Roller diameter and gear ratio in Motor Config (defaults 80 mm and 108), plus the stop angle at Max RPM.
- The manual workpiece diameter survives a restart.
- About shows the build commit next to the version.

## Fixed

- Leaving Calibration restores the panel pot and pedal. They stayed ignored until restart after MOVE 360 from Settings.
- The program editor limits RPM by the program's own diameter.
- Jog releases no longer look active, and the header shows STOPPING during deceleration.
- Pulse shows the RPM it will run and reports START BLOCKED. Step speed and angle buttons act only when idle, and Step shows fractional angles.
- The accent color applies to the main speed card, and the light scheme is readable.

## Known limits

These cannot be solved in firmware alone:

- An open E-STOP wire reads as healthy, and E-STOP acts through the MCU and the driver ENA input. It does not disconnect motor power.
- A floating pot wiper is not detected, and the analog pedal is not jump-checked.

## Validation and hardware status

- **511 native/control/speed/storage/safety test cases** and the packaging tests pass.
- Linux and Windows simulator self-tests and layout audits pass.
- Release, debug and mirror firmware compile.
- **Not tested on the machine.** No stop-time, calibration-accuracy or HF measurements are claimed.

Check on the machine after flashing:

1. Press E-STOP during a settings save at rest.
2. Run a calibration, then check the pot and pedal.
3. Use touch and the pedal during TIG HF, and watch for false **HMI STALE** or pedal faults.

## Upgrade

Existing settings and programs are retained with the matching partition layout, and a standard application-image update is sufficient. Check roller diameter and gear ratio in Motor Config, and re-check any program that uses a large diameter.
